HOW TO DROP YOUR ORIGINAL SVG LOGOS & TEAM PORTRAITS:

A. LOGOS:
1. Look at the File Explorer on the left side of your AI Studio interface.
2. Locate the "public" folder, and inside it, the "assets" folder.
3. You can right-click the "assets" folder and select "Upload File", or drag and drop your SVG files directly into it!
4. Please name them:
   - "logo-lime.svg" (The Lime Green background variant with Hot Pink text)
   - "logo-pink.svg" (The Hot Pink background variant with Lime Green text)

B. TEAM PORTRAITS (If you want to replace the custom animated avatars):
1. Drop your custom portraits (either PNG or SVG) into the same "public/assets" folder.
2. Please name them:
   - "lara.svg" or "lara.png"
   - "mayra.svg" or "mayra.png"
   - "tomy.svg" or "tomy.png"
   - "cami.svg" or "cami.png"
   - "caro.svg" or "caro.png"

The app is fully configured to support either SVG or PNG formats for these portraits. Let me know once you've uploaded them, and we're good to go!
